Photography, for me, begins where words are no longer enough. It is a way of holding onto an emotion, a silence, a fleeting presence. I feel particularly attuned to its artistic language, to the delicate search for balance, harmony, and emotion within each frame. More than simply creating images, I try to capture what can be felt, and perhaps allow that feeling to resonate beyond myself.
